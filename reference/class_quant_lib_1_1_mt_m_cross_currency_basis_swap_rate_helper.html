Detailed Description

Rate helper for bootstrapping over market-to-market cross-currency basis swaps.

Helper for a cross currency swap with resetting notional. This means that at each interest payment the notional on the MtM leg is being reset to reflect the changes in the FX rate - reducing the counterparty and FX risk of the structure.

For more details see: N. Moreni, A. Pallavicini (2015) FX Modelling in Collateralized Markets: foreign measures, basis curves and pricing formulae.

paymentFrequencypayment frequency of the base-currency leg; if left unset (the default) the schedule is derived from the base-currency index tenor.
paymentLagpayment lag, in days, applied to both legs (default: 0).
quoteCurrencyPaymentFrequencypayment frequency of the quote-currency leg; if left unset (the default) it defaults to paymentFrequency, and if that is unset as well the schedule is derived from the quote-currency index tenor.

In both frequency parameters, NoFrequency is accepted as a synonym for an unset (null) value.
