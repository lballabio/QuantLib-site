Detailed Description

Sum-of-exponentials approximation of the fractional kernel.

Approximates the power-law kernel

\[ K(t) = \frac{t^{\alpha - 1}}{\Gamma(\alpha)}, \qquad \alpha \in (0, 1], \]

by \( K^n(t) = \sum_{i=1}^n c_i e^{-x_i t} \). Following Abi Jaber, K is completely monotone with Bernstein representation

\[ K(t) = \int_0^\infty e^{-xt} \mu(dx), \qquad \mu(dx) = \frac{x^{-\alpha}}{\Gamma(\alpha)\Gamma(1-\alpha)} dx , \]

and a partition \( 0 = \eta_0 < \eta_1 < \ldots < \eta_n \) gives non-negative \( c_i, x_i \) — hence a \( K^n \) that is itself completely monotone — by collapsing each interval's mass onto its barycentre,

\[ c_i = \int_{\eta_{i-1}}^{\eta_i} \mu(dx), \qquad x_i = \frac{1}{c_i}\int_{\eta_{i-1}}^{\eta_i} x\, \mu(dx). \]

Taking \( \eta_0 = 0 \) recovers the non-fractional limit exactly: as \( \alpha \to 1 \) the first interval absorbs all the mass, so \( c_1 \to 1 \), \( x_1 \to 0 \) and \( K^n \to K \equiv 1 \), with no special-casing.

References:

E. Abi Jaber, Lifting the Heston model, Quantitative Finance 19(12), 1995-2013 (2019).

E. Abi Jaber and O. El Euch, Multifactor approximation of rough volatility models, SIAM Journal on Financial Mathematics 10(2), 309-349 (2019).

Tests
the weights and rates are tested for non-negativity, for convergence of \( K^n \) to \( K \) as \( n \) grows, and for the exact \( \alpha = 1 \) collapse to a single node.

Both endpoints have to widen with n: refining n never subdivides the leading interval \( [0, \eta_{lo}] \), so holding it fixed stalls the error at a few percent, and \( \eta_{hi} \) is what represents the singularity at the origin. The exponents are empirical: the relative \( L^1 \) kernel error, measured for \( H \in \{0.05, 0.1, 0.3\} \) and \( n \le 160 \), falls off like \( n^{-1.55} \).
